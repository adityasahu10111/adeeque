## Design System — Bold Editorial
- Bg: white or cream — typography IS the design
- Palette: black + white + ONE accent color only (cobalt, red, or chartreuse)
  Accent used once per section — restraint is the point
- Typography: extreme size contrast is mandatory
  Hero headline: clamp(60px, 10vw, 120px), line-height 0.9
  Caption/label: 11–12px, all caps, letter-spacing 0.2em
  Mix: serif for editorial moments, grotesque sans for UI elements
- Layout: asymmetric magazine columns — avoid centered or symmetric grids
  Some sections are literally just one big word and small text
- Dividers: thick horizontal rules (4–6px solid black) — no decorative shapes
- Avoid: multiple colors, rounded corners, playful/bouncy design, SaaS layouts
  Never use more than 2 typeface styles in one project
